# Review Process

Status: Draft

## Purpose

Define how proposed HC-SPEC content is reviewed before it becomes accepted or canonical.

## Process

1. Draft the proposal.
2. Mark it as proposed when ready for review.
3. Review technical consistency, epistemic consistency, terminology, security implications, and compatibility with existing specifications.
4. Record disagreements rather than forcing artificial consensus.
5. Resolve or explicitly document blocking objections.
6. Accept, reject, or return the proposal for revision.
7. Promote accepted material into canonical documents when appropriate.

## Review principles

- Preserve provenance of important objections and decisions.
- Distinguish factual disagreement from design preference.
- A reviewer may approve a document while recording non-blocking concerns.
- Canonical documents should link to the RFC or ADR that materially established them.
- Automated agent review may assist humans but does not silently promote content to canonical state.
